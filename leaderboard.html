How it works on event day

The Live Leaderboard runs on a real-time database, so every device showing this page stays in sync within seconds — no refreshing needed.

👥

Commissioner Entry

The Commissioner (and any approved assistants) signs in to a private admin page to enter scores as each hole is completed. Everyone else only sees the leaderboard — not the controls.

🔒

Hidden from Players

The Commissioner can keep the leaderboard hidden from players during the round to preserve the suspense. Until they tap "Reveal," anyone on this page sees a placeholder. Spectators not playing can still follow along if revealed early.

↑↓

Position Changes

When a team overtakes another or falls behind, a green up arrow or red down arrow appears beside their rank — easy to spot at a glance.

📡

Offline Catch-Up

If you lose signal mid-round, the leaderboard catches up the moment you reconnect.
