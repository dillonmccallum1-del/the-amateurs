Rules & Scoring

2026 Footgolf Edition

General Rules

  • Every hole is played as a two-person alternate kick. Partners take turns striking each successive shot. Alternate who tees off on each hole.
  • After your fifth shot as a group, pick up, record a 6 (double par) on the scorecard, and move to the next hole.
  • Everyone plays from the forward footgolf tee box.
  • Avoid the water on holes #2 and #9 — there's no way to retrieve a ball once it goes in.
  • Each group's bag holds a "Guess the Grass" sheet asking which species of turfgrass is in the cup. No phones. Submit it to the Commissioner before the awards ceremony.
  • Many holes feature special games — details revealed on event day.
  • Have fun.
Heads up: Holes #2 and #9 carry water hazards with no recovery. Lay up if you must.

Scoring

The Amateurs uses a modified Stableford system — the team with the highest point total wins the MacCallum Cup. Score your card normally as if playing stroke play. The Commissioner tallies points at the end of the tournament.

Result Points
Hole−in−One +20
Birdie +5

Bogey +1
Double Bogey 0
Triple Bogey (double par) −2
