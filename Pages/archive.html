Final standings

Pulled live from the leaderboard. The History card shows the top 3 places (champion + runners-up); the full list is saved for the stats tables. Ties share a place (e.g. “T-3”).

Champion photo

The big winners' photo shown at the top of the year card and on the Home page. Uploads as soon as you pick it.

Superlatives

Award name + who won it. The winner box suggests every player — pick from the list or type any name (e.g. multiple people). Add as many as you like; blank rows are ignored.

Highlight photos

Thumbnails for the year's gallery. Pick several at once, or add a few at a time — each uploads immediately and stacks up below, so you can keep adding.

Full album (Google Drive)

A link to the Drive folder with all the photos and videos from the year. It shows on the History card as a “View full album” button, so people can browse everything beyond the highlight thumbnails above.

Tip: in Drive, set the folder to “Anyone with the link” so family can open it without signing in.
